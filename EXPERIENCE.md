## Overview

I build products from an initial idea through implementation, release and ongoing improvement. My work spans web interfaces, APIs, background processing, developer tools and published iOS applications.

I started developing software around 2007, initially building tools for data collection, processing and peer-to-peer file sharing. I later worked on web applications, education platforms and database optimization for data-intensive systems, including roles at TulaCo, Intolimp and Scand. I then spent seven years at Ethereum Swarm building decentralized storage applications, TypeScript libraries and developer tools. Since then, I have focused on independent products spanning AI-agent orchestration, video processing, developer utilities and native applications.

I think beyond implementation: who a product serves, how people discover it, what helps them get value and whether the service is practical to operate. My product work includes exploring organic discovery and paid acquisition, as well as improving the experience after someone arrives.

My strongest areas are:

- **AI-agent orchestration:** breaking development work into concrete tasks, coordinating execution and checking results through tests and working software.
- **Video and media engineering:** connecting generation, editing, captions, rendering and export into usable workflows.
- **Product engineering:** taking responsibility for both the user experience and the systems behind it.
- **Developer infrastructure:** building SDKs, command-line tools, APIs and reusable components.
- **Data-intensive applications:** decentralized storage, data processing pipelines and asynchronous operations.

## How I Work

### Product ownership

I care about the complete product: the problem it solves, how people discover it and what happens when they try to use it.

Building my own products has involved interface design, backend development, media-processing costs, positioning and user acquisition. I consider both organic discovery and paid channels, and treat acquisition as something to investigate and improve.

I connect these questions to engineering decisions: what to build first, where to simplify the experience and which improvements are worth the implementation and operating costs.

### AI-assisted engineering

I use AI agents as part of an engineering process that includes clear tasks, relevant context, review and validation.

When an approach does not work, I investigate the assumptions and redirect the work. For research-heavy media projects, I have used reference-behavior tracing and deterministic checks to evaluate successive implementations.

My contribution includes deciding what to build, choosing how to investigate it and determining whether the result is ready to use.

### Reusable systems

Several of my projects turn a recurring problem into a reusable tool: coordinating development agents, processing media, managing storage or simplifying a developer workflow.

I look for components and interfaces that can support more than one application while keeping the immediate product usable.

### Areas of Interest

My experience is particularly relevant to teams building:

- AI products, agent orchestration and workflow automation.
- Video, audio and creator tools.
- Web applications with substantial backend or processing requirements.
- Developer platforms, SDKs and infrastructure.
- Data-intensive and financial applications that need clear interfaces, dependable integrations and testable workflows.

For financial products, my relevant background is in software engineering, data infrastructure and blockchain-connected applications.
